Silk and needle

The handful of ideas that explain how Hornet fights, heals and gets stronger.

Silk

The resource

Fuels healing and special attacks. You build it by landing needle hits, so fighting well keeps you stocked.

Bind

Healing

Spend Silk to restore health. It costs nine Silk and restores three masks, and a hit taken mid-bind wastes the lot.

Silk Skills

Special attacks

Powerful moves paid for with Silk, such as Silkspear.

Crests

Gear sets

Each crest changes Hornet's moveset and how many tool slots she has. You begin with the Hunter Crest, a well-rounded pick for early play.

Tools

Fill the crest slots

Red tools are active gadgets, blue tools are passive defence, and yellow tools are passive boosts for offence or exploration.

Rosaries and Shell Shards

Money and refills

Rosaries are the currency for shops. Shell Shards refill your limited-use red tools at a bench.

Masks and Spools

Health and Silk capacity

Collect four mask shards for one extra mask of health. Spool fragments raise the most Silk you can hold.

Bellways

Fast travel

Stations that let you hop back across the map. The first opens after you free the Bell Beast in The Marrow.

Up through Pharloom

The early and middle regions in the order most players meet them. The path can vary, and the later regions are left for you to find.

  1. Moss Grotto

    The first area after Hornet escapes her cage, thick with green moss.

  2. The Marrow

    Moss gives way to rocky, bone-like ground as the air starts to heat up.

  3. Deep Docks

    A hot place with lava glowing behind armoured enemies.

  4. Far Fields

    Rising lava currents create updrafts, and the cooler air supports more living things.

  5. Greymoor

    A large region of crumbling towers and armed foes.

  6. Bellhart

    A region full of bells, including a platforming stretch that uses them.

  7. Shellwood

    A greener area of plants and fisherfolk.

  8. Blasted Steps

    A barren, windswept climb across sandstorm country.

The Citadel waits at the top, with more beyond it.

Faces on the road

Travellers, merchants, rivals and old friends from both games. These notes steer clear of late-game spoilers.

Pharloom

Sherma

Pilgrim

A devout pilgrim whose road keeps crossing Hornet's as she climbs. Gentle and sincere, he is one of the warmest faces in a dangerous kingdom.

Shakra

Hunter and mapmaker

An ally who knows Pharloom's ground well. She charts the way for others and does not hesitate to fight when it matters.

Lace

Fencer

Keeps turning up between Hornet and the Citadel. Their meetings build a sharp, personal rivalry carried mostly by conversation.

Garmond and Zaza

Wandering hunters

A pair of hunters who travel Pharloom together. Meet them on the road and see where their paths lead.

Pinmaster Plinney

Pin tool merchant

Sells the pin tools Hornet uses for ranged attacks. If you want to fight from a distance, his stall is the place to start.

The Bell Hermit

Crest merchant

Deals in crests and the slots they fit into, the gear that changes how Hornet fights.

Sprintmaster Swift

Movement trainer

Offers training that sharpens Hornet's movement, for anyone who wants to cross Pharloom faster.

Mister Mushroom

A cameo from Hallownest

A wandering fungal traveller who returns from the first game, a small reminder of where Hornet came from.

Hallownest

The Knight

The Vessel

The silent, bug-like hero of the first game, who explores Hallownest with a nail and very little explanation.

Zote the Mighty

Self-declared warrior

A boastful fighter with a wooden nail and a bottomless supply of self-praise. Often found in trouble, and always ready with another speech.

Cornifer

Cartographer

A travelling mapmaker who charts each region as he goes, humming while he works. Finding him is the first step to knowing where you are.

Iselda

Map seller

Runs a small shop in Dirtmouth selling maps and the supplies for marking your own. Cornifer's partner in keeping the kingdom charted.

Quirrel

Scholar

A learned, courteous wanderer who studies Hallownest's history and appears at several points on the Knight's journey. He is Monomon's apprentice.

Elderbug

Dirtmouth resident

The last resident of Dirtmouth. He welcomes the Knight back each time and shares what he remembers of the town.

Sly

Shopkeeper

A Dirtmouth shopkeeper eager to sell the Knight something useful in exchange for Geo.

Salubra

Charm seller

A merchant who sells charms and extra charm slots, tucked away in the Forgotten Crossroads.

Grimm

Troupe Master

The showman at the head of a travelling troupe, whose arrival turns a corner of Hallownest into a stage.

Questions

Quick answers, including how to put your own models on this page.

Is Silksong a sequel?

Yes. It is Team Cherry's follow-up to Hollow Knight, and this time you play Hornet.

Do I need to play Hollow Knight first?

Silksong takes place in a new kingdom with its own journey. The first game gives helpful context for Hornet and for cameos like Mister Mushroom, and many players start there, but it is up to you.

Where can I play it?

On PC, Nintendo Switch, Xbox and PlayStation. It released on 4 September 2025.

How do I put my own Hornet model on this page?

Save your model as models/hornet.glb, or point modelUrl in the config block at the top of the page to wherever it lives. Use modelScale to change the size, modelOffset to nudge it, and modelRotationY to choose which way she faces.

You can also drag a .glb onto the model window to try a file without editing anything.

How do I add another model?

Use the round window at the bottom of the page. Set secondModel.url in the config block, along with its title, description and scale. The needle drifting in the background can be swapped the same way with backgroundModel.url.

What happens when I click the model?

It gives a little jump, spins faster for a moment and throws out a burst of stars.

Is this the official site?

No. It is an unofficial fan page and is not connected to Team Cherry.
